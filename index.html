Welcome to Our School

Welcome to PM SHRI Rupahi Higher Secondary School, a center of academic excellence and holistic education. Our institution is committed to nurturing young minds through quality education, strong moral values, and a supportive learning environment.

At PM SHRI Rupahi Higher Secondary School, we focus on the overall development of students by encouraging intellectual growth, discipline, creativity, and social responsibility. Our dedicated and experienced teachers guide students to achieve academic success while developing confidence, leadership skills, and a lifelong love for learning.

We provide a well-structured curriculum, modern teaching methods, and a safe, inclusive campus where every student is valued and inspired to reach their full potential. Through academics, co-curricular activities, and community engagement, we prepare our students to face future challenges with knowledge and integrity.

We warmly welcome students, parents, and visitors to be a part of the Rupahi Higher Secondary School family..

Message from the Academic In-Charge

Welcome to Rupahi Higher Secondary School. Our aim is to provide quality education that nurtures knowledge, discipline, and moral values among students. We believe that education is not only about academic excellence but also about character building and lifelong learning.

With the support of dedicated teachers and the cooperation of parents, we strive to create a positive learning environment where every student can grow, succeed, and contribute meaningfully to society.
